# 0.5.1 — 2026-11-03

## Added

- **`earthquake_get_event` `include: ['pager']`** (USGS) returns the PAGER fatality and economic-loss alert levels with every probability bracket (economic bounds in millions of USD), the impact statements, and the people exposed at each shaking intensity, in total and per country (`UK` marks areas assigned to no country). It costs two or three extra requests; an event whose PAGER product predates the JSON files, or has none, lists the group as `not_published`. ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/64))
- **`min_mmi` and `min_cdi`** on `earthquake_search_events` and `earthquake_count_events` floor the ShakeMap and DYFI intensities (1–12, sent as `minmmi` and `mincdi`). Both are USGS-only: an EMSC call names them in `ignoredFilters`, and an event without a ShakeMap or DYFI reports never matches. ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/65))
- **`notice`** on `earthquake_count_events`, and the empty-page notice of `earthquake_search_events`, name each `event_type`, `magnitude_type`, or `product_type` value USGS does not list, with the listed value it likely meant (`moment_tensor` for `moment-tensor`). It applies to a USGS count of zero or an empty first search page, and reads USGS's type list with one extra request, cached for 24 hours. ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/66))

## Changed

- **`earthquake-guide` skill** (1.1) documents the `pager` group and the `mmi` null, and its examples cite settled events and closed, re-runnable windows. ([#67](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/67), [#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/64), [#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/63))
- **`REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS`** descriptions (config, `server.json`, `manifest.json`, `.env.example`, README) state that every feed, search, count, and event call runs under one 50-second deadline. ([#59](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/59))

## Fixed

- **`earthquake_count_events`** runs under the 50-second call deadline on both sources and settles as `source_timeout` within it; 0.5.0 allowed four attempts of `REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` plus backoff, past a client's 60-second timeout when the setting exceeded about 13,000 ms. A search's count lookup still gets what remains of the search's deadline. ([#59](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/59))
- **A 2xx body that is not the expected document** (truncated JSON, plain text, `null`, `{}`) on feed, search, count, and event lookups, for both sources and the resources, makes one upstream request and fails as `source_unavailable` (`feed_unavailable` for feeds) with a fixed message; 0.5.0 retried four times and returned parser or engine text. A connection dropped mid-body still retries and fails without engine text. ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/60))
- **Records missing a value the source always publishes** fail the same way: a USGS or EMSC feature with no ID, origin time, update time, or readable position, or a feed with no `metadata.generated`. 0.5.0 answered with `emsc-unknown-*` IDs and `1970-01-01` timestamps. ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/61))
- **USGS `status`** is `null` when absent or not `automatic`, `reviewed`, or `deleted`; 0.5.0 reported `automatic`. ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/61))
- **USGS `mmi: 0`**, the placeholder for a ShakeMap that computed no maximum intensity, is reported as `null` on feed, search, and event results, matching `detail.shakemap`. ([#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/63))
- **`event_type`** list members are trimmed before sending; `earthquake, explosion` sent ` explosion`, which USGS matched against nothing. ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/earthquake-mcp-server/issues/66))
